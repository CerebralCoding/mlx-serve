# diffusion_gemma plugin

Owns `diffusion_gemma` (mlx-community's layout): the Gemma 4 26B-A4B trunk under `model.decoder` generated by block
diffusion, served (`served.zig`). The engine's runner (`runner.zig`, `scheduler_arm.zig`) drives the loop over the
plugin's forwards (bit-equal to tapes recorded from the legacy ones; the served loop commits the legacy tokens). The
legacy forward is deleted: a pack the plugin declines is refused by name. The image tower under `model.encoder` is not
served (text only, as the engine).

## Files
- `config.zig`: MLX-free claim + parse (Gemma's `Config` with the Gemma 4 traits, K=V on full layers, the canvas
  facts from `canvas_length` and the embedded `generation_config`, its stop set when the root has none).
- `plugin.zig`: the table over Gemma 4's load, forward and `trunk_weight` (the self-conditioning reads the table),
  `conformanceFixture`; `proof_test.zig`: encoder passes and canvas steps (4-bit, mxfp4) and the canvas loop vs
  tapes recorded from the legacy forward.
- `runner.zig`, `scheduler_arm.zig`: the engine's canvas loop (moved in P1), driving the served forwards.
- The canvas forward itself is `gemma4/decoder.zig canvas` (it shares the MoE tail and the weights).

## Facts the table declares
- `block_diffusion` (`.canvas` declared, `canvas` facts), no batched rows, no exactness contract
  (`.bb/reports/exactness-declarations.tsv`: drafted/resumed n/a, concurrent unverified).

## Rules (moved from root AGENTS.md)
- Block diffusion: PLD, drafters, MTP, batching and the prefix cache never apply; instruct-only.
- Every non-canvas forward is the causal ENCODER pass: the encoder's own layer scalars
  (`model.encoder.language_model.layers.N.layer_scalar`, the only untied encoder text params).
- A canvas step reads the cache, never writes it: q/k/v of the canvas alone at the committed length, sliding layers
  see the last `window - 1` committed keys, one unmasked bidirectional read; the unfused MoE tail; logits every row.
- Self-conditioning: `rms(embed + down(geglu(pre_norm(signal))), ones)`; with no signal the weight-less norm still runs.
- Parity = converged-canvas self-consistency on real packs (`MLX_SERVE_DIFFUSION_TRACE=1`).
